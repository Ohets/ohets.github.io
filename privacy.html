Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains which personal data is processed when you use Air Simulator and how you can exercise your data protection rights.

1. Data Controller and Contact

The operator of Air Simulator is responsible for processing the data.

Email: steho01234@gmail.com

2. What Data Is Processed?

  • Account and sign-in: When you create an account or sign in, the information you provide, particularly your email address and authentication data, is processed to provide sign-in and manage your account.
  • Game progress: Air Simulator stores game information such as credits, unlocked aircraft, and completed missions locally in your browser storage (localStorage) so your progress is retained.
  • Freiwillige Updates per Email: Wenn du dich für News und Updates anmeldest und zustimmst, wird deine E-Mail-Adresse samt Einwilligungsstatus gespeichert, um dir entsprechende Nachrichten zu senden. Diese Anmeldung ist freiwillig.
  • Technical data: When you visit the website, hosting or technical service providers may process standard connection data, such as your IP address, request time, and device/browser information. Which log data is stored and for how long depends on the hosting provider used.
  • Support: If you send an email, the information in your message is processed to respond to your request.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

Account data is processed to provide sign-in and manage the game account. Where processing is necessary to provide the features you request, it is based on performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Optional email updates are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example by emailing steho01234@gmail.com. The lawfulness of processing before withdrawal is not affected.

Technically necessary security and operational data may be processed where required to provide and protect the service securely and where legitimate interests under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR apply.

4. Service Providers and Recipients

Supabase is used for authentication and storing optional update sign-ups. Data required for these features is transmitted to Supabase. Please also see Supabase Privacy Policy.

If you click a PayPal link, you will be redirected to PayPal. Any processing there is carried out by PayPal under its own privacy policy. See PayPal Privacy Policy.

Personal data is not intentionally sold. Other recipients may include technical hosting and infrastructure providers where necessary to operate the service.

5. Data Retention

Local game saves remain in your browser until you delete them or remove website/browser data. Account and sign-in data are generally processed for as long as the account or relevant feature is needed, unless statutory retention obligations or other lawful reasons require longer storage. Exact deletion periods for data held by service providers depend on their configuration and applicable agreements; the operator must verify and document these periods.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Air Simulator uses localStorage to save game progress. The authentication service may use necessary storage mechanisms for sign-in and session management. You can delete stored data through your browser settings; doing so may cause you to lose saved progress or your sign-in session.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, or restrict processing of your data, to data portability, and to object, subject to the applicable legal requirements. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future.

To exercise your rights or ask questions, contact steho01234@gmail.com.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if data processing or legal requirements change. The currently published version applies.
